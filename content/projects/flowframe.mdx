Runners and hikers track every step, but seeing the data means looking down at a wrist or stopping to unlock a phone. FlowFrame puts it in your line of sight on smart glasses instead. You set up the run or hike on your phone; while you move, the glasses show only what you need in the moment, and when you stop the full record waits on your phone.
